one homepage, three models

Three ai models, fable 5.1, opus 5.5 and fable 5.2, were each asked to design the same personal homepage for daymi.dev. These are all 18 pieces they produced: finals, sketches and the ones they dropped. Each column is one model and each row one stage, so read across a row to compare. Every piece shows its desktop capture (1440×900) with the phone capture (390×844) on top. Click a capture to enlarge it, or run any design live, full screen here or in a new tab. The projects on every page are invented placeholders.

The benchmark prompt, which opus 5.5 and fable 5.2 got word for word 
build my personal homepage for daymi.dev. it's the first thing people see when they look me up. boil the ocean.

me: daniel kistner, "daymi", always lowercase. lives in the beautiful black forest. studies applied computer science at hochschule offenburg, working student at frei gmbh. builds things with and for ai agents. bio: "building stuff, i guess". github.com/itsdaymi, x.com/itsdaymi, daniel@daymi.dev. voice: dry, understated, no hype.

needs: hero, projects (data-driven, use 4-6 plausible placeholders), short about, links, tiny footer.

i want good taste, slick physical motion, and a layout nobody has seen on a portfolio before. one unforgettable idea, everything else quiet. no generic ai-site look.

static html/css/js, no build step. responsive to 360px, accessible, reduced motion respected.

hill climb: explore several directions, pick the strongest, then screenshot at desktop and phone, critique it like a picky design lead, and iterate until it's genuinely great. don't stop at good.

Read this before comparing

  • Not a fair fight. Fable 5.1 had subagents and its own brief, opus 5.5 ran alone for under an hour per round, and fable 5.2 had two outside reviewers and a day and a half.
  • Only opus 5.5 and fable 5.2 got the identical prompt. Both landed on the black forest and both sketched a hanging mobile: opus built it, fable dropped it for the forest.
  • Every page uses placeholder projects its model invented, so ignore the project names.

How the pages were kept

  • The files from the first two runs were deleted along with their repo. They were rebuilt from the agents' own edit history, replayed in order, and every page loads without errors.
  • Self-hosted fonts were fetched again from the same open-source font sources the agents used.
  • Captures labelled as the model's own are originals. The rest were taken fresh in headless chrome at 1440×900 and 390×844.

Missing or different

  • The first, stuttgart version of tageslicht. Fable 5.1 overwrote it in round 2.
  • daymi<T> differs slightly from the model's own capture, because three late edits could not be replayed.
  • Werkstatt's about line was reworded to name the employer as an affiliation only.
  • Opus 5.5's 12-font specimen page and the agents' planning notes are not included.
